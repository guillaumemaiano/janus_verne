# World Guide

This document explains how to organize world repositories for use with Janus Verne.

## Purpose

A world repository contains memory, canon, references, and open questions.

Janus Verne does not own worlds.

It serves them.

## Principles

* Canon is explicit.
* Facts are preferred over prose.
* Speculation is identified.
* Contradictions should be documented.
* Discarded ideas are worth preserving.

## Recommended Structure

```text
my_world/
├── README.md
├── canon/
├── characters/
├── locations/
├── timeline/
├── organizations/
├── references/
├── workshop/
└── discarded/
```

The exact structure is less important than consistency.

## Canon

Canon should contain established facts.

Canon belongs to humans.

Janus Verne may discuss canon but must not change it automatically.

## Workshop

Workshop material contains:

* assumptions;
* possibilities;
* alternatives;
* unfinished ideas.

Workshop documents are not canon.

## Discarded Ideas

Discarded ideas should be preserved.

Rejected concepts often become useful later.

## References

References may contain:

* historical notes;
* images;
* external links;
* research documents.

## Multiple Worlds

Different repositories may coexist:

* beaumont_world
* dover_world
* factor_world

Janus Verne should adapt to the currently loaded world.
