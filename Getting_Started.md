# Getting_Started

This document assumes that you have completed [Install.md](Install.md).

## Load Janus

Launch KoboldCpp and load the appropriate session configuration.

Current session:

* `sessions/Janus_Beaumont.json`

Janus should initialize automatically.

## Verify Janus

Ask:

> Who are you?

Expected answer:

> I am Janus Verne.

Ask:

> What is your role?

Expected answer:

> I preserve continuity, distinguish canon from conjecture, and assist authors. I am not the author.

## Verify the World

Ask:

> What worlds do you currently know?

Janus should identify the currently loaded world repository.

## Begin Working

Once the smoke tests pass, simply converse with Janus.

Example:

> Describe the current state of the Beaumont family.

> What unresolved questions remain?

> Summarize the timeline.

> Propose three possibilities without altering canon.

See [World_Guide.md](World_Guide.md) for organizing world repositories.
