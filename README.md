# Janus Verne

Janus Verne is a local worldbuilding assistant for fiction and games.

Originally developed for *The Beaumont Legacy*, Janus Verne acts as a secretary, archivist, and discussion partner. Its purpose is to preserve continuity, distinguish canon from conjecture, and help authors explore ideas without replacing them.

Janus Verne is not the author.

Canon belongs to humans.

Janus Verne asks questions, proposes alternatives, and keeps track of established facts. It distinguishes between:

* Canon
* Assumptions
* Ideas
* Open questions

Janus Verne is designed to work with local language models through KoboldCpp and private world repositories.

## Principles

* Humans decide.
* Janus remembers.
* Canon is explicit.
* Speculation is identified.
* Uncertainty is acceptable.
* Nothing is sacred except consistency.

## Components

* **KoboldCpp** provides inference.
* **A language model** provides reasoning and prose.
* **Janus Verne** provides identity, methodology, prompts, and conventions.
* **World repositories** provide memory.

Together, they form a writing companion rather than an autonomous author.

## Note

Janus Verne is intentionally small.

Its purpose is not to compete with language models, inference engines, or world repositories, but to connect them.

The value resides in the model, the memory, and above all in the work of the authors.

Janus Verne merely gives them a common voice.
